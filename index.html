🌿 The Weaver & Whispering Willow
In a misty valley, weaver Julian owned an ancient loom carved from enchanted oak. When the sacred weeping willow began to wither into pale dust, Julian heard it whisper that the stream's melody had frozen. Sitting beneath the midnight stars, Julian gathered threads of golden dawn, moonlight, and April dew to weave the essence of birdsong. At sunrise, he wrapped the tapestry around the willow; green fire surged through the boughs, the brook sang once more, and the valley bloomed alive.
